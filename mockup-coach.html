Your body is not asking for the threshold session today; it is asking to be listened to, and then trained gently around its edges.

The picture you describe — recovery 38%, RHR up 4 bpm, HRV down 17 ms below baseline — is not random noise. It is a coherent signal of an autonomic nervous system still metabolising last night's strain of 18.4, which sat in your top decile for the month. Eight hours of sleep is necessary, but here it was insufficient: your body needed closer to nine and a half to fully clear the load.

Rather than a binary train / don't train verdict, here is the calibrated path I would take in your position today:

  1. Move the threshold session to Wednesday. A 48-hour delay puts it on the back of an expected green recovery and protects the quality of the work — a sub-quality threshold piece is worse than no threshold piece.
  2. Substitute a Zone 2 aerobic block today, 50–65 minutes, heart rate capped at 142 bpm. This drives mitochondrial adaptation without further depressing HRV.
  3. Front-load protein and electrolytes at lunch — 35–45 g protein, 1 g sodium. Yesterday's run likely under-replenished, which is part of the autonomic story.
  4. Lights down by 21:30 tonight. Your sleep onset latency has been creeping up; an earlier wind-down should restore the missing 80 minutes.

If recovery climbs above 62% by Wednesday morning, the threshold piece is on. If it stalls below 50%, the conversation we should have is about cumulative load over the past nine days, not about a single session.

Conversational pace. You should be able to recite a paragraph aloud without gasping — and feel slightly bored by minute twenty.

On a depressed-HRV day, true Zone 2 will arrive at a lower heart rate than usual and at a higher perceived effort. Trust the breath, not the watch: nasal-only inhale through most of the session is your honest ceiling. If you find yourself opening the mouth to breathe, you have drifted into Zone 3 and the session stops being restorative.

Listen for: warmth in the hands by minute eight, light sweat by twelve, no burning in the legs at any point. The session ends when you feel you could comfortably do another twenty minutes — not when the timer expires.
